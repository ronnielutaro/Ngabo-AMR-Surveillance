# ADR 0010 — v0.1 Resistance-Profile Similarity Policy

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Decision Owner:** Ngabo maintainer  
**Scope:** Hackathon v0.1.0 synthetic AMR surveillance demonstration  
**Policy Version:** `ngabo-profile-sim-v1`  
**Algorithm Version:** `exact-ratio-v1`  

---

## Context

Ngabo's signal-detection pipeline (Milestone 2, Issue #45) evaluates antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) data to identify phenotypic clusters across isolates. Downstream agentic reasoning and Proof-Carrying claims (ADR 0009) require machine-verifiable findings to establish whether isolates exhibit identical or divergent resistance patterns.

Prior to explicit maintainer approval, early prototype drafts described the comparison ratio and minimum panel threshold ($k=3$) without a governing architectural decision record. Independent review of PR #95 identified that turning an agent-selected heuristic into deterministic scientific authority requires explicit maintainer governance, clear boundary definitions, and formal separation from clinical validation.

To preserve scientific integrity, safety boundaries, and the zero-human Taskmaster workflow, the Ngabo maintainer has established a formal policy governing resistance-profile comparison for the v0.1.0 demonstration.

---

## Decision

Adopt the **Ngabo v0.1 Prototype Resistance-Profile Similarity Policy** (`policy_version = "ngabo-profile-sim-v1"`, `algorithm_version = "exact-ratio-v1"`).

The policy enforces the following ten maintainer-approved rules:

1. **Organism Compatibility**: Only isolates with the same canonical `organism_code` are comparable. Heterologous organism comparisons fail closed symmetrically with status `INCOMPATIBLE_ORGANISM` (similarity score is `None`).
2. **Comparable AST Panel**: The comparable AST panel is the intersection of antimicrobials for which **BOTH** isolates have a known interpretation: `SUSCEPTIBLE` (`S`), `INTERMEDIATE` (`I`), or `RESISTANT` (`R`).
3. **UNKNOWN Exclusion**: Observations with interpretation `UNKNOWN` are strictly excluded from the comparable denominator.
4. **Untested Exclusion**: Untested or missing antimicrobials in either isolate are strictly excluded from the comparable denominator. Neither UNKNOWN nor untested observations are ever invented into susceptible or resistant calls.
5. **Exact Agreement Ratio**: Similarity is calculated as the exact ratio of identical categorical calls over comparable known calls:
   $$\text{Similarity} = \frac{\text{identical } S/I/R \text{ calls}}{\text{comparable known calls}}$$
6. **Minimum Panel Threshold**: At least 3 comparable antimicrobials are required ($\text{comparable} \ge 3$). If fewer than 3 comparable antimicrobials are shared, the comparison returns status `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` (similarity score is `None`).
7. **Serialization Precision**: Numerical similarity values are serialized to 4 decimal places (e.g., `1.0000`, `0.3333`).
8. **Phenotype Scope**: The calculation represents resistance-phenotype / profile similarity only.
9. **Non-Clinical Limitations**: The calculation does **NOT** establish genomic relatedness, strain identity, transmission, epidemiological linkage, outbreak probability, outbreak confirmation, diagnosis, or treatment guidance.
10. **Prototype Status**: The minimum panel size of 3 and this similarity method are **prototype Taskmaster demonstration policy/configuration**, **NOT** clinically validated thresholds or standards.

---

## Explicit Characterization & Governance Disclaimers

This policy and its implementation are explicitly designated as:

- a **prototype surveillance heuristic** designed for synthetic workflow demonstration;
- a **synthetic demonstration** configuration optimized for the hackathon scenario;
- a **deterministic and reproducible** mathematical comparison of recorded observations.

It is explicitly:

- **NOT** clinically validated;
- **NOT** outbreak probability;
- **NOT** transmission evidence;
- **NOT** genomic relatedness;
- **NOT** diagnosis;
- **NOT** treatment guidance.

Neither Ngabo nor any downstream agent may present an exact-ratio similarity finding as an externally validated clinical standard or as proof of an epidemiological outbreak.

---

## Deterministic Code Ownership vs. Model Bounded Reasoning

Under Clean Architecture (ADR 0003) and Proof-Carrying Reasoning (ADR 0009):

1. **Deterministic Code Owns the Calculation**:
   - The computation of resistance profile similarity is pure arithmetic over canonical AST observations.
   - LLM generation is non-deterministic, vulnerable to calculation error, and unable to guarantee identical hash outputs across invocations.
   - Allowing Gemini to compute or estimate similarity scores from isolate prose would bypass machine-verifiable integrity checks and violate the core invariant of Issue #45:
     > *Resistance-profile findings are pure, reproducible, versioned calculations over canonical AST observations; UNKNOWN and untested values never become invented susceptibility results.*
   - Therefore, the pure domain service (`compute_profile_similarity`) computes the immutable `ProfileSimilarityFinding`.

2. **Gemini Must Reference, Not Recompute**:
   - In Proof-Carrying synthesis, Gemini receives pre-computed, immutable `ProfileSimilarityFinding` objects in its execution context.
   - When Gemini asserts isolate similarity in a reasoning draft, it must construct a typed `DERIVED_FINDING` claim that directly references the finding's opaque cryptographic ID (`psim-...`) via `DeterministicFindingReference`.
   - The model must **REFERENCE** the resulting finding rather than attempting to recompute, round, or re-evaluate the ratio in free text.
   - The deterministic claim verifier validates that the referenced `finding_id` exists, matches the canonical input records (`input_refs`), and corresponds to an accepted policy version before the claim can support any autonomous A1 action.

## Cryptographic Finding Identity & Collection Semantics

To ensure referential integrity and fail-closed safety:

1. **Complete-Content Binding**:
   The stable finding ID (`psim-<sha256[:16]>`) binds to a canonical JSON representation (sorted keys, compact separators) containing all material finding data:
   - `policy_version` (`"ngabo-profile-sim-v1"`)
   - `algorithm_version` (`"exact-ratio-v1"`)
   - `config_version` (`"min3-strict-org-v1"`)
   - `input_refs` (canonical lexicographically sorted isolate IDs)
   - `organism_code` (shared organism code or `None` if incompatible)
   - `status` (string value of `ProfileSimilarityStatus`)
   - `comparable_antibiotics` (sorted tuple of comparable codes)
   - `matching_antibiotics` (sorted tuple of matching codes)
   - `differing_antibiotics` (sorted tuple of differing codes)
   - `untested_or_unknown_antibiotics` (sorted tuple of uncompared codes)
   - `similarity_score` (formatted to `similarity_precision` or `None`)
   - `similarity_precision` (integer precision)
   - `output_value` (canonical serialized output string)

2. **Symmetric Pair Ordering**:
   `compare_canonical_isolates(a, b)` and `compare_canonical_isolates(b, a)` yield value-identical findings and identical finding IDs. Pairwise references are always ordered lexicographically by isolate ID.

3. **Fail-Closed Duplicate Collection Semantics**:
   When evaluating a collection of isolates (`compare_isolate_collection`):
   - Exact value-identical duplicates sharing an `isolate_id` collapse idempotently.
   - Conflicting `CanonicalIsolate` objects sharing an `isolate_id` **fail closed** by raising `ValueError` (no first-wins, no last-wins).

---

## Consequences

- **Positive**: Eliminates ambiguity regarding scientific authority in v0.1.0; establishes reproducible, verifiable AST profile comparison; aligns domain logic with ADR 0009 Proof-Carrying Reasoning.
- **Boundaries**: Explicitly restricts similarity findings to phenotype agreement without clinical or genomic overreach.
- **Future Work**: Post-hackathon clinical validation, genomic ML models, or alternative distance metrics (e.g., Euclidean MIC vectors) will require new versioned policies (e.g., `ngabo-profile-sim-v2`) and corresponding ADRs.
