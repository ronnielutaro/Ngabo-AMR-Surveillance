# ADR 0011 — v0.1 Temporal and Location Concentration Policy

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Decision Owner:** Ngabo maintainer  
**Scope:** Hackathon v0.1.0 synthetic AMR surveillance demonstration  
**Policy Version:** `ngabo-concentration-v1`  
**Config Version:** `win7d-org-facility-ward-v1`  
**Temporal Algorithm Version:** `retrospective-count-v1`  
**Location Algorithm Version:** `ward-share-v1`  

---

## Context

Ngabo's signal-detection pipeline (Milestone 2, Issue #46 / Epic #18) evaluates temporal clustering and spatial (ward) concentration across canonical isolate observations. Downstream agentic reasoning and Proof-Carrying claims (ADR 0009) require machine-verifiable findings to quantify isolate accumulation over time and geographic concentration within healthcare facilities.

Issue #46 specifically owns descriptive **measurements** of temporal count and location proportion. Downstream Milestone 2.7 (Issue #47) owns signal thresholds, baseline excess calculations, composite signal scoring, and investigation candidate triage.

To preserve scientific rigor, Clean Architecture (ADR 0003), and zero-human Taskmaster autonomy (ADR 0007 / ADR 0009), the Ngabo maintainer has established a closed, maintainer-approved policy governing temporal and location concentration calculations for the v0.1.0 demonstration.

---

## Decision

Adopt the **Ngabo v0.1 Prototype Temporal and Location Concentration Policy** (`policy_version = "ngabo-concentration-v1"`, `config_version = "win7d-org-facility-ward-v1"`).

The policy enforces the following eleven maintainer-approved rules:

1. **Measurement-Only Responsibility**: Issue #46 produces factual measurements. It does **NOT** enforce a minimum threshold (such as $k=3$) to declare success. Cohorts containing 1, 2, or more isolates are valid measured data. Issue #47 owns investigation thresholds and signal significance.
2. **Explicit Analysis Window**: Calculations **MUST NOT** access `datetime.now()`, `date.today()`, system clocks, UTC conversions, or timezones. `window_end` is a required date-only (`datetime.date`) input. The 7-day retrospective analysis window is defined as `[window_start, window_end]` where `window_start = window_end - 6 calendar days` (both boundaries inclusive).
3. **Two Distinct Finding Types**: Calculations yield two separate, typed finding models:
   - `TemporalConcentrationFinding` (retrospective isolate count and span)
   - `LocationConcentrationFinding` (ward proportion of facility isolates)
   They are never collapsed into an opaque composite score in Issue #46.
4. **Temporal Concentration Policy (`retrospective-count-v1`)**:
   - Grouping key: `(organism_code, facility_id)`.
   - Computes `facility_organism_count`, `observed_min_date`, `observed_max_date`, and `observed_span_days` ($(\text{max\_date} - \text{min\_date}).\text{days} + 1$).
5. **Location Concentration Policy (`ward-share-v1`)**:
   - Grouping key: `(organism_code, facility_id, ward)`.
   - Computes `ward_organism_count` and `facility_organism_count` across all wards in the facility window.
   - Computes `location_concentration_ratio = ward_organism_count / facility_organism_count`.
   - Serialized to exactly 4 decimal places (`precision = 4`).
   - Evaluates all observed wards in the facility window without suppressing low-count wards.
6. **Authoritative Input Authority**:
   - Every record materially used in a finding must be referenced in its proof reference.
   - For temporal findings: `input_refs` includes all same-organism/facility records in the window.
   - For location findings: `ward_input_refs` records numerator isolates; `facility_window_input_refs` records denominator isolates. The authoritative `DeterministicFindingReference.input_refs` **MUST** be the union of all material records (`facility_window_input_refs`), because denominator records directly determine the ratio.
7. **Deterministic Cryptographic Finding Identity**:
   - Separate prefixes: `tconc-<sha256[:16]>` for temporal findings and `lconc-<sha256[:16]>` for location findings.
   - Finding IDs hash canonical JSON (sorted keys, compact separators, UTF-8) binding all material finding content.
8. **Duplicate Safety**:
   - Identical `CanonicalIsolate` objects sharing an `isolate_id` collapse idempotently.
   - Conflicting `CanonicalIsolate` objects sharing an `isolate_id` **FAIL CLOSED** (`ValueError`).
9. **Order and Window Determinism**:
   - Input sequence permutations do not affect finding values, IDs, or collection ordering.
   - Findings are deterministically sorted: temporal by `(organism_code, facility_id)`; location by `(organism_code, facility_id, ward)`.
10. **Closed Policy Configuration**:
    - `window_days = 7` and `precision = 4` are fixed under v0.1 policy. Runtime configuration spoofing or mutation is disallowed.
11. **Non-Clinical / Non-Outbreak Boundaries**:
    - Findings represent descriptive temporal accumulation and location share only.
    - Findings do **NOT** establish outbreak probability, confirmed cluster, transmission, epidemiological linkage, spread, clinical severity, or treatment guidance.

## Missingness & Boundary Analysis

In Ngabo's Clean Architecture, `CanonicalIsolate` (Issue #38) already enforces that:
- `collection_date` is a date-only `datetime.date`;
- `organism_code`, `facility_id`, and `ward` are non-blank text strings.

Therefore:
- `MISSING_WARD`, `MISSING_FACILITY`, and `MISSING_COLLECTION_DATE` are structurally impossible for accepted canonical records.
- The only material missingness state in Issue #46 is `EMPTY_DENOMINATOR` (when a query is executed for a facility cohort with zero isolates in the window, preventing division by zero).

---

## Status Taxonomy

- `ConcentrationStatus.SUCCESS`: Measurement calculated over valid observations.
- `ConcentrationStatus.INSUFFICIENT_DATA`: Data insufficient to compute ratio (e.g., `EMPTY_DENOMINATOR`).
