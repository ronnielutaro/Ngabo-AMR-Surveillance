NGABO-AUTHORED RETRIEVAL/INDEXING SUMMARY (not a CDC quotation).

CDC-CRE-001 defines carbapenem-resistant Enterobacteriaceae (CRE) as
Enterobacteriaceae nonsusceptible to a carbapenem or documented to produce a
carbapenemase.

Retrieval categories: CRE case definition, carbapenem resistance,
carbapenemase production, laboratory recognition.
