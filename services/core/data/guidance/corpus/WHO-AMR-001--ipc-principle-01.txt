NGABO-AUTHORED RETRIEVAL/INDEXING SUMMARY (not a WHO quotation).

WHO-AMR-001 covers infection prevention and control (IPC) practices and
procedures for carbapenem-resistant Enterobacteriaceae, Acinetobacter
baumannii and Pseudomonas aeruginosa in acute health care facilities.

Retrieval categories: hand hygiene, contact precautions, personal protective
equipment, environmental hygiene, cohorting, healthcare worker education, and
facility-level IPC coordination to prevent and control transmission.
