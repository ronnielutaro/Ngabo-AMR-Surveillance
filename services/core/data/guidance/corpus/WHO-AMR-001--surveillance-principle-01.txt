NGABO-AUTHORED RETRIEVAL/INDEXING SUMMARY (not a WHO quotation).

WHO-AMR-001 addresses early recognition and laboratory surveillance of
carbapenem-resistant Enterobacteriaceae, Acinetobacter baumannii and
Pseudomonas aeruginosa in acute health care facilities.

Retrieval categories: early recognition, laboratory detection, surveillance
for CRE, and IPC coordination at national and facility level.
